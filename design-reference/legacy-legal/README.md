# Legacy legal copy (not built)

These are the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and SMS Terms from the previous
Next.js site, kept only so the wording isn't lost when those pages get built in
Astro. Nothing here is compiled — `design-reference/` is outside `src/`.

**They do not match the current offer.** Before porting any of it, note that
this copy carries:

- $497 / $697 pricing and a $500 setup fee (now $299/mo standard, $149/mo
  current rate, $299 one-time setup)
- a founding-client **money-back guarantee including a refunded setup fee** —
  the current brief forbids the words "money-back guarantee", "full refund",
  "refundable" and "satisfaction guaranteed" anywhere on the site
- a 1,500-minute fair-use clause and a $0.35/min overage rate
- a 7-day data-return commitment and a 90-day call-recording retention window

The parts worth carrying over largely unchanged are the SMS disclosures
(STOP/HELP, frequency, rates, the no-sharing-of-mobile-opt-in sentence) and the
Colorado Privacy Act rights section, since those were written against carrier
requirements rather than against the old pricing.
